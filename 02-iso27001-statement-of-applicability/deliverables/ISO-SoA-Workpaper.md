## Applicability corrections

A.7.5 and A.7.8 are applicable. Remote company-managed laptops remain exposed to environmental damage, unsafe placement, theft and loss. The lack of premises excludes facility controls, but does not remove the duty to protect in-scope endpoint equipment.
