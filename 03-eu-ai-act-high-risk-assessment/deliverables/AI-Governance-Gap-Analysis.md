# TalentMatch focused AI governance gap analysis

This is a focused readiness analysis, not an ISO/IEC 42001 certification audit.

| Area | Scenario position | Required evidence / priority |
|---|---|---|
| Context/interested parties (ISO 4; MAP 1.1,5.1) | Candidates, recruiters and 150+ customers are known. | Affected-group, intended/prohibited-use, legal-context and impact record. Critical. |
| Leadership/policy (ISO 5; GOVERN 1.1–1.4) | Provider role indicated; accountable leadership not evidenced. | AI policy, RACI, risk tolerance and deployment gate. Critical. |
| Planning/risk/impact (ISO 6; MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE) | Seven risks recorded, effectiveness unknown. | Measurable fairness, accuracy, privacy and oversight objectives plus approval of treatment. Critical. |
| Resources/competence (ISO 7; GOVERN 2.2) | Recruiters decide; training evidence absent. | Recruiter/engineering training and accessible guidance records. High. |
| Lifecycle/data operation (ISO 8; A.6.2.3) | Historical data, NLP/XGBoost and SHAP stated. | Lineage, feature governance, versioning, TEVV and release criteria. Critical. |
| Measurement/audit (ISO 9; MEASURE 1.2,2.1,2.5) | 78% agreement only; no fairness/drift/oversight results. | Metrics, thresholds, independent testing, monitoring and audit record. Critical. |
| Improvement/incidents (ISO 10; MANAGE 2.2,3.2) | No CAPA, complaint or management-review evidence. | Incident/appeal process, corrective action and review evidence. High. |

Sequence: establish governance gate; complete data/proxy/performance testing; redesign and measure human review/recourse; then operate monitoring and corrective action before reassessing risks. No AIMS certification is claimed.
