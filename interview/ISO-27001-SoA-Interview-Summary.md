# ISO 27001:2022 SoA — CloudNative Analytics Interview Summary

**Organization:** CloudNative Analytics Ltd.  
**Scope:** Remote-first AWS SaaS platform, managed laptops, customer data in AWS `eu-west-1`.  
**Assessment:** `ded16f7a-7e97-43bd-8422-b3bfc5787a18`.

The SoA reviews all 93 Annex A controls. Applicability is separate from implementation evidence and compliance conclusion. Applicable controls without sufficient evidence remain **Not assessed**; workflow completion does not claim certification.

| Demonstration topic | Explain | Show in CISO Assistant |
|---|---|---|
| A.8.1 User endpoint devices | Remote staff use company-managed laptops; endpoint protection addresses theft/loss risk R-002, but implementation evidence must be verified. | ISO audit → A.8.1 observation and linked risk. |
| A.5.23 Cloud services | AWS is fundamental to customer-data processing; supplier/cloud governance is applicable to R-001, R-003 and R-005. | ISO audit → A.5.23 and CloudNative risk register. |
| Physical exclusions | No offices or data centres does not eliminate endpoint/home-working risk; exclusions are limited and alternative controls are documented. | ISO audit → excluded A.7 entries and workpaper exclusion defenses. |

Avoid saying “ISO certified.” Say: “I completed an educational SoA review, linked the six source risks, and documented implementation evidence gaps for follow-up.”
